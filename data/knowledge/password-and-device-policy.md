# Password and Device Policy

Owner: Information Security · Last review: 2026-08-15

## Passwords
Passwords must have at least 14 characters. Use the company password manager (Bitwarden) for all work passwords. Passwords are not rotated on a fixed schedule; change a password immediately if you suspect it was exposed.

## Laptops
Company laptops use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes. Install software only from the self-service portal. Report a lost or stolen laptop within 1 hour through an ITOPS ticket with priority "Critical" and inform the security team in the Teams channel #security.

## Private devices
Private devices may be used for email and Teams only through the managed apps. Patient data must never be stored on private devices.
