# Using AI Tools at YourDoctor

Owner: AI Enablement · Last review: 2026-10-01

Approved AI tools are the internal assistant and the company workspace of the approved chat tool. Other AI services are not allowed for work data.

Rules:
- Never paste patient data into an AI tool unless the tool pseudonymises it automatically.
- AI output is a draft. A human checks it before it is sent to patients, doctors or authorities.
- If the assistant cannot find an answer in the internal documents, it says so. Ask the document owner instead of guessing.
- Report wrong or harmful AI answers in the Teams channel #ai-feedback.

New AI use cases are proposed through a Jira ticket in the project AIENABLE. They are assessed for value, effort, data sensitivity and risk before development starts.
