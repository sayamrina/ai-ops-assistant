# IT Access and Multi-Factor Authentication

Owner: IT Operations · Last review: 2026-09-01

## Requesting access
Access to internal tools (Jira, Confluence, SharePoint, Zoho CRM) is requested through a Jira ticket in the project ITOPS with the type "Access request". The request must name the tool, the role needed and the business reason. The line manager approves the ticket before IT grants access.

## Multi-factor authentication
MFA is mandatory for every account. Use the Microsoft Authenticator app. SMS codes are not allowed because they can be intercepted.

## Lost or new phone
If you lose your phone or get a new one, open an ITOPS ticket with priority "High" and the type "MFA reset". IT verifies your identity by video call before resetting MFA. Never share MFA codes with anyone, including IT staff.

## Leaving the company
Accounts are disabled on the last working day at 18:00. Managers create the offboarding ticket at least five working days in advance.
