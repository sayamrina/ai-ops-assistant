# Patient Support: Refunds and Cancellations

Owner: Patient Support · Last review: 2026-07-20

## When a refund is possible
A consultation can be refunded in full if no dermatologist has opened the case yet. Once a dermatologist has started the assessment, refunds are only possible if the case could not be answered for medical reasons (for example, the photos were not usable and the patient did not respond to a request for new photos within 7 days).

## How to process a refund
Create the refund in Zoho Billing with the reason code. Refunds above 150 EUR need approval from the Support Lead. The patient receives the money within 5 to 10 business days.

## Insurance invoices
Patients with private insurance receive an invoice according to the German fee schedule (GOÄ). Questions about reimbursement by the insurer are answered with the template "Insurance reimbursement".
