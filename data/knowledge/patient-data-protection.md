# Handling Patient Data (GDPR)

Owner: Data Protection Officer · Last review: 2026-09-10

## Principles
Patient data is health data under Art. 9 GDPR. Access follows the need-to-know principle. Only use patient data in the systems approved for it: the medical platform and the support tool. Do not copy patient data into email, chat, Confluence or AI tools.

## AI tools
Internal AI tools may process patient-related text only after pseudonymisation: names, email addresses, phone numbers, dates of birth, insurance numbers and IBANs must be removed or replaced. External AI services may only be used if a data processing agreement is in place.

## Data subject requests
Requests for access (Art. 15), correction (Art. 16) or deletion (Art. 17) must be forwarded to privacy@yourdoctor.example on the same day. The legal deadline to answer is one month from receipt. Do not confirm or deny to the requester whether a person is a patient before identity is verified.

## Data breaches
Any suspected data breach must be reported to the Data Protection Officer immediately and at the latest within 24 hours, so that the company can notify the authority within 72 hours if required.
