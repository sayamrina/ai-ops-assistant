# Onboarding New Dermatologists

Owner: Medical Operations · Last review: 2026-06-30

New dermatologists send an application with their medical licence (Approbation), specialist certificate in dermatology, professional liability insurance and their IBAN for payouts. Medical Operations verifies the licence with the regional medical chamber before the account is activated.

Onboarding steps:
1. Verify documents (licence, specialist certificate, liability insurance).
2. Sign the cooperation contract in DocuSign.
3. Create the platform account and the training account.
4. Complete the platform training (about 2 hours) before the first case.

Missing or expired documents put the application on hold. Medical Operations informs the applicant within 3 working days.
