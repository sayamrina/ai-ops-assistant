# Patient Support: Prescription Questions

Owner: Medical Operations · Last review: 2026-08-02

Support staff never give medical advice. Questions about the dosage, side effects or the choice of medication are forwarded to the treating dermatologist through the medical platform.

If a pharmacy cannot read or accept an e-prescription, support checks the prescription status in the platform. If the prescription is valid, send the patient the e-prescription code again. If it is invalid, forward the case to Medical Operations with priority "High".

Prescriptions are valid for 28 days. After that the patient needs a new consultation.
